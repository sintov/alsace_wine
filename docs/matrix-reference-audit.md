# Matrix reference audit — 19 September 2026

## Scope and interpretation

All 51 Grand Cru records were checked against their CIVA site profiles; the unavailable Altenberg de Bergbieten profile was replaced by Domaine Frederic Mochel's vineyard description. Grape lists contain positive evidence from the profiles (planting lists and explicit descriptions of site wines), not all legally permitted varieties and not a current planting census. Explicit extra plantings at Brand are included; vague statements such as “all Alsace varieties” are not expanded into invented lists.

All seven regional views are derived from individual supported site/grape/geology tuples. A grape somewhere in a region and a soil somewhere else in that region do not establish a combination. Regional coverage is incomplete, especially outside Grands Crus. The other existing named-site records were not all independently re-researched in this pass.

The default view hides unsupported axes, not proven-impossible combinations. Full view exposes unknown combinations as ?, site-reference geology mismatches as ×, sourced examples as ○ and tasted journal records as ✓. User records, wishlist wines, blend hints, targets and exclusions retain their axes. A recorded, sourced parcel correction takes precedence over whole-site geology. The display does not certify appellation eligibility.

## Material corrections

- Altenberg de Bergbieten: added the missing gypsum-marl/limestone reference and Riesling, Gewurztraminer, Pinot Gris and Muscat evidence.
- Distinguish marl–sandstone and limestone–sandstone from marl–limestone–sandstone. This avoids asserting a third component absent from the classification. Existing category IDs remain valid for old targets and parcel corrections.
- Cleebourg product references replace unsupported village-scale assignments for six named sites: Keimberg is described as silt/sand/clay, Kammerberg and Eselsforch as clay-limestone; Hannesacker, Huettgasse and Brandhof lack a geology description in their current product pages. Their exact communes are left unverified. Existing wine entries remain intact, but reference-derived geology and coverage can change to reflect these corrections.
- Mixed categories are coarse whole-vineyard references; they are not proof that every grape is planted on every constituent rock. No pure-component coverage is manufactured from them.
